Homework & Interactive Practice

Theme: Family & Friendship | Level: A2 | Target Skills: Reading, Vocabulary, Chunks, Grammar & Writing

1
Reading Passage: A Friendship That Lasts

"My name is Adam, and my best friend is Yusuf. We have known each other for many years. We met at school, and we still keep in touch.

Yusuf is more outgoing than me, but we have a lot in common. We both enjoy reading, travelling and spending time with our families.

We talk on the phone every week. I can always count on Yusuf when I need help. A good friendship is about trust and respect."

1. How do Adam and Yusuf stay connected?

2. How is Yusuf different from Adam?

3. What is a core foundation of a good friendship according to Adam?

2
Vocabulary & Target Chunks

Fill in the blanks with the correct preposition or target chunk.

1. I get along well my colleagues at work.
2. We share hobbies; we have a lot common.
3. When I have a problem, I can always count my best friend.
4. Even after moving to another city, we try to keep touch.
5. A friend who is always ready to support you when you are in trouble is .
3
Grammar: Comparative Adjectives
Short Adjectives: adj + -er + than
Long Adjectives: more + adj + than
1. My brother is than me.
2. My best friend is than me.
3. My sister is than my brother.
4. A teacher needs to be than a tourist.
4
Writing Production: Answer • Explain • Example

Prompt: Describe someone important in your life (a family member or a friend).

Use the 3-step expansion strategy:
Step 1: State who they are & character trait (e.g., supportive, reliable, outgoing).
Step 2: Compare them with someone or explain why (e.g., ...more patient than... / I can count on...).
Step 3: Give a specific everyday example (e.g., For example, whenever I need help with...).
